The Eye of the Sahara

Discover one of Earth's most mysterious geological formations, known as the Richat Structure - a circular marvel visible from space.

Learn More

A Natural Wonder

The Eye of the Sahara is a circular geologic feature in the Sahara desert that resembles a massive bullseye when viewed from space.

Geological Marvel

Located in Mauritania, this 40-kilometer wide structure was initially thought to be an impact crater but is now believed to be a deeply eroded dome.

Ancient Formation

Estimated to be between 485 and 400 million years old, this geological wonder has fascinated scientists and visitors alike for generations.

Visible From Space

So perfectly circular that it's easily visible from orbit, astronauts have used it as a landmark when passing over the Sahara desert.
